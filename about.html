About Our Scented Soy Candles 🕯️

100% Organic Soy Wax Candles

Welcome to The Melting Point. Crafted with emotion and poured with love, our candles are completely handcrafted using natural soy wax. We use premium aromatic oils designed to relieve stress and bring a warm, inviting luxury scent into your home. Because we use organic materials, our candles give a clean burn without any black smoke.

🌿 Eco-Friendly & Safe: Our natural soy formulation is completely toxin-free and safe for indoor atmospheres. Unlike paraffin wax, it releases zero harmful emissions when burnt correctly according to safe standard guidelines.

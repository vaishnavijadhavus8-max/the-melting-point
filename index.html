⚠️ Candle Safety Regulations & Care Guide

To ensure a beautiful and hazard-free experience, please adhere strictly to our fire safety guidelines:

1. Burn Sight

Never leave a burning candle unattended. Always extinguish candles completely before leaving the room or going to sleep.

2. Safe Placement

Keep burning candles away from flammable items like curtains, paper, decorations, clothing, and open draft windows.

3. Clear Zones

Keep burning vessels completely out of reach of children, pets, and crowded high-traffic tables or shelves.

💡 Pro-Tip for Terracotta & Metal Bowls: Always trim your candle wick to 1/4 inch before every lighting session. This prevents excessive soot, controls excessive flame heights, and ensures a clean, long-lasting 30+ hour burn.
